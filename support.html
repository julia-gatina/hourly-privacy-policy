Frequently asked questions

How do I start tracking a shift?

Tap the clock-in button on the main screen. Your elapsed time and earnings update live while you work. Tap it again to clock out, and the shift is saved with a full pay breakdown. If you take an unpaid break, start a break while clocked in and that time is excluded from your pay.

I forgot to clock in. Can I add a shift afterwards?

Yes. You can add a shift for any past date by hand, and edit or delete any shift you have already recorded.

I closed the app while clocked in. Did I lose the shift?

No. A running shift is restored when you reopen the app, including the time that passed while it was closed.

Why does my pay estimate not match my actual paycheck?

That is exactly what the comparison feature is for. Enter what you were actually paid for a pay period and Hourly shows you the difference against its own calculation. If the two do not match, the usual causes are a pay rule that needs adjusting - an overtime threshold, a differential, or a deduction - or a genuine error on the paycheck. Check your pay rules first, then take the figures to your employer.

How do I set up overtime?

Overtime rules vary by province and state, so nothing is assumed. Open your job's settings and set your own daily and weekly overtime thresholds and multipliers. The same screen holds your pay period type, shift differentials and pay rate.

Can I track more than one job?

Yes. Add as many jobs as you need, each with its own pay rate, rules, colour and pay period. Totals are kept separate, and grouped by currency where they appear together.

Estimated take-home looks wrong.

The net figure is an estimate based on the deductions you configure, which you can set as percentages or fixed amounts. It cannot know your exact tax situation, so treat it as a guide rather than a guarantee.

How do I get my data out?

You can export any pay period as a CSV file through the standard share sheet - mail it to yourself, save it to Files, or send it wherever you need it.

Is my data backed up?

Not by us. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. There is no account and no server, which means we cannot see your data - and equally cannot recover it for you. Deleting the app deletes your data. If your device backs itself up to iCloud, your Hourly data may be included in that backup.

Can I remove the ads?

Not at the moment. Hourly is free and supported by a single banner ad. On iOS you can decline ad tracking when asked, or later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; ads still appear, they are simply less personalised.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The only thing that needs a connection is loading the ad.
